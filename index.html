Kenya Wildlife Service

The Kenya Wildlife Service (KWS) manages Kenya's national parks and reserves. We protect wildlife and their habitats for current and future generations.

What We Do

  • Patrol parks and reserves against poaching
  • Monitor wildlife populations
  • Manage conflict between humans and wildlife
  • Run conservation education programmes
  • Issue permits for research and tourism
